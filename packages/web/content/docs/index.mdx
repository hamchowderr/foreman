# Welcome

Foreman is an AI assistant that takes actions across 9,000+ apps through your
Zapier account. Tell it what to do in plain English — it picks the right app
and action, shows you the draft, and runs it after you approve.

This site is a quick tour of what Foreman can do and how to get set up.

## Where to start

- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) — connect your Zapier account and run your first action.
- [Channels](/docs/channels) — use Foreman from Slack, Discord, Telegram, and more.
- [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) — run Foreman on your own box.
