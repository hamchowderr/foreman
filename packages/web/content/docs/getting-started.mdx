---

Three steps and you're running.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in

    Head to [foreman.otakusolutions.io](https://foreman.otakusolutions.io) and
    click **Sign in**. We use Clerk, so you can use email, Google, or GitHub.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Connect your Zapier account

    On first launch, Foreman asks for access to your existing Zapier account.
    One click and you're connected — we don't store your password, just a
    revocable access token (encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM).

    <Callout type="info">
      Everything you've already wired up in Zapier — Gmail, Slack, Trello,
      any of the 9,000+ apps — is instantly available. Nothing new to set up
      on their side.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Just say it

    Try any of these:

    - *"What apps do I have connected?"*
    - *"Email Jake the Q3 deck."*
    - *"Create a Trello card for client follow-up in my To Do list."*

    For every write action, Foreman shows you a draft and waits. Nothing
    runs until you hit **Approve**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Does Foreman see my email inbox?">
    Only if you ask it to. It uses your Zapier connection, so it can see
    whatever you've already authorized Zapier to see — no more, no less.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if Foreman picks the wrong app?">
    Every action has an approval step. You see the full draft before it
    runs. If it's wrong, hit **Decline** and rephrase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Foreman without connecting Zapier?">
    You can sign in and chat, but no actions will actually run. Zapier is
    the connection layer — without it there's nothing to automate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it work with my existing Zaps?">
    Yes. Foreman uses your Zapier account alongside whatever Zaps you
    already have. It doesn't modify them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What about rate limits?">
    30 actions/minute per user, 200/hour. Bulk operations over 5 records
    get an extra confirmation. See [Guardrails](/docs/core-concepts/guardrails).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
