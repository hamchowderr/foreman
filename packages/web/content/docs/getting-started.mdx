# Getting started

Three steps and you're done:

## 1. Sign in

Head to [foreman.otakusolutions.io](https://foreman.otakusolutions.io) and
click **Sign in**. We use Clerk, so you can use email, Google, or GitHub.

## 2. Connect your Zapier account

On first launch, Foreman will ask to connect your Zapier account. It's one
OAuth click — we don't store your Zapier credentials, just the access token
(encrypted with AES-256-GCM, revocable from Zapier at any time).

## 3. Just say it

Try one of these:

- *"What apps do I have connected?"*
- *"Email Jake the Q3 deck."*
- *"Create a Trello card for client follow-up in my To Do list."*

For every write action, you'll see a draft and a big **Approve** button.
Nothing runs until you say so.
